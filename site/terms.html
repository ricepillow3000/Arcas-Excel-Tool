Free and open source

Arcas is a free Excel add-in made by John Nguyen. Its code is open source under the MIT License, so you may use, copy and change it, at home or at work, for any number of people, at no cost.

Nothing is charged for Arcas. There is no price, no plan, no trial that turns into a bill, and no payment details are ever asked for or collected. So there is nothing to refund, and no refund policy is needed. If AI help ever costs you anything, it is Google charging you on your own Google key, never John charging you: the privacy page says when that can happen.

No warranty

Arcas is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind, as the MIT License says. John is not liable for any loss or damage that comes from using it, to the fullest extent the law allows.

That last part matters: nothing written here takes away a right the law where you live does not let John take away. In some countries that includes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, and some protections given to consumers. Where the law and this page disagree, the law wins and the rest of this page still stands.

Check before you rely on it

Arcas can flag correct values and miss mistakes. A suggestion changes nothing until you accept it. You are responsible for checking each change before you accept it, and for checking important figures yourself before you rely on a sheet it has looked at.

AI suggestions

Ask AI, Check words with AI and Check subject rules with AI are optional. Their suggestions come from Google's Gemini and can be wrong, even when they sound sure. Read each one before you accept it.

If what you type reads like someone in trouble, the pane answers with helpline numbers instead of asking the AI, and sends nothing anywhere. That is a signpost, not a crisis service, and Arcas is not one: in an emergency call your local emergency number.

Invite codes

An invite code is for you alone: don't share it, post it or sell it. Each invite has a daily cap, and John's relay is for help with Excel fixes only. Don't try to get around the caps, send other work through the relay or overload it. John can turn off any invite at any time, and there is no promise the relay is up.
