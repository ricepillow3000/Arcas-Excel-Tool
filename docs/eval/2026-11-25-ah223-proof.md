# AH-223: does the scan adapt to the sheet, its groups and its user? (2026-11-25)

John's goal: "a self-adapting learning algorithm that knows what it's getting itself into and what it's looking at,
and adjusts its bias and rules for that user", so users stop worrying about old data and focus on new data. The ML
engineer's four points were checked against the code, and each gap was measured, fixed, then measured again.

Run it yourself: `python backend/bench/proof_ah223.py backend` on this branch, and the same script pointed at a
checkout of main (`ed35b1a`) for the "before" column. Synthetic sheets only: a big group of 400 rows, a small one of
40 at another scale, a lost decimal point (x0.1) and an extra zero (x10) planted in each, 10 seeds a subject.

## What the engineer said, and where it stands

| Advice | Before today | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Rules catch the obvious, learning only the rest | Already so: hygiene checks decide alone, the statistical detectors only vote | Unchanged |
| 2. Judge normal within each group | Only when groups were 10x apart, never by a region | 2x apart, a region included, a person's own group never |
| 3. Careful learning from clicks, checked by group | A Dismiss teaches one exact value in one column; no group scorecard | Same rule, now proven against a lazy clicker; `bench/groups.py` grades every change by group |
| 4. Show why, let users set sensitivity | Every flag says why; the strength slider | The reason names the group ("for Region South"); Before you scan says "Salary judged within each Region." |

## 1. It says what it is looking at

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Before you scan, payroll by region | "This sheet looks like a table." | "This sheet looks like a table. Salary judged within each Region." |
| The small region's lost decimal point | "Salary baseline limit is 29000; this is 13811.67" (Noted, not flagged) | "Salary 13811.67 is far outside the other Salary values for Region South (weird limit 40000), 10 times smaller than a usual one: a slip of zeros or the decimal point" (Medium) |

## 2. It bends its rules for each group (strength 5, the default)

| Subject | Big group caught | Small group caught, before | Small group caught, after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retail prices (Gadget 3x Widget) | 18 → 20 of 20 | 10 of 20 | 20 of 20 |
| Payroll by region (South 2.5x North) | 19 → 20 of 20 | 10 of 20 | 19 of 20 |
| Shipping by mode (Air 4x Ground) | 18 → 20 of 20 | 10 of 20 | 20 of 20 |
| Clinic waits (Satellite 0.4x Main) | 17 → 19 of 20 | 20 of 20 | 20 of 20 |

False alarms on honest rows: 0.00% in every group, before and after.

At strength 8 the small groups catch 20 of 20 in both versions, the big groups 19-20 before and 20 after; the small group's honest rows flagged fell from 0.53% to 0.00%
(retail), 0.26% to 0.00% (payroll) and 0.53% to 0.26% (clinic), the big group's at most 0.05% throughout.

## 3. It learns from the user, at once

A value the user dismissed is quiet on the next scan, before and after (Medium → none). This branch first broke that
for a value judged within its group (it stayed Medium); the proof caught it, `scale` joined `ONE_CELL` in the engine and
the pane, and `test_a_value_dismissed_where_its_group_judged_it_is_quiet_on_the_next_scan` holds it.

## 4. Lazy clicks cost nothing

The user dismisses every flag on the sheet, then 4 new slips land (2 in each group): 2 of 4 are caught after the lazy
clicks, and 2 of 4 with nothing learned at all, so the clicks took away nothing. The 2 missed are not the learning's:
each new x0.1 lands beside an old one (10.04 beside 10.7), and two values that close read as a real cluster. That
limit is older than this work and is left as it is.

## The guard rails

- Industry bench 51 of 51 caught, blind bench 199 of 201, 0 Medium+ false alarms on both: unchanged.
- `backend/bench/groups.py` runs in `tools/check.py` and CI: a small group caught 10 points less often, or falsely
  flagged 1 point more, than its big group fails the build.
- A sex, race or age group never sets a normal of its own (`test_a_persons_own_group_never_sets_a_normal_of_its_own`).
- 10 new mutants, each killed. CI's mutation job also caught one more: with each account keeping its own normal, a
  real journal entry 100x its account's usual size, debits equal to credits, became "a slip of zeros" (Medium). The
  per-group check now skips every cell its row's arithmetic or its record's balance confirms
  (`test_a_balanced_entry_100_times_its_accounts_usual_size_is_worth_a_check_never_a_slip`).

## What this is not

Nothing is trained, and nothing carries from one workbook to the next. The scan adapts to each sheet from that sheet's
own numbers (which column splits it into groups, each group's median and spread) and to what its user dismissed or
stopped on that sheet. That is the design: the same sheet gives the same answer on any PC, and a user's clicks on one
file never quietly change another.
